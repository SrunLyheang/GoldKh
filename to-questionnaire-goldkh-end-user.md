# GoldKh End-User Discovery Questionnaire

**Purpose:** GoldKh tracks a user's personal gold holdings (bought/sold in Cambodian units — chi, damlung) against the live spot price, showing weighted average cost and unrealized gain/loss. Before deciding what to build next, we need to hear from someone who'd actually use it day-to-day — what matters, what's missing, and whether the gold-buying details we've assumed match how it really works.

**From:** Lyheang (building GoldKh) — **To:** you, as a prospective GoldKh user — **How your answers will be used:** to prioritize the next set of features and fix any gaps between how the app models gold transactions and how it actually works for real buyers/sellers.

## Context

GoldKh is a web app, not an exchange — no money moves through it. You sign in, log your gold buys and sells (quantity, unit, price paid, date), and the dashboard shows your total holdings, average cost, current market value, and gain/loss. It's currently open to public signup. We want to know if this matches how someone who actually buys/sells gold would want to track it.

## How to answer

No hard deadline — whenever you have 15-20 minutes. Answer inline under each question. "I don't know" or "doesn't apply to me" is a fine answer — just say so rather than skipping.
